Hello, glow-getter

Created with imagination. Inspired by confidence.

JAZZNIM GLOW BEAUTY is an original, fictional beauty brand concept developed for an HTML and CSS creative web development activity. The idea is to present an online product showcase with a calm, feminine visual identity and a collection of invented beauty products.

Our concept celebrates everyday self-care without promising perfect skin or one definition of beauty. We believe a glow is simply the confidence you bring to your day.
